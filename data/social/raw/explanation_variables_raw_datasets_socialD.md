## `mss_index_x.csv`

| Variable | Explanation |
|---|---|
| `plr_key` | number of the Planungsraum (8-digits) |
| `plr_name` | name of the Planungsraum |
| `res_count` | count of residents |
| `s1_unempl_status_indicator` | status indicator measuring the share of unemployed persons in the planning area at the reference date |
| `s2_single_parent_child_status_indicator` | status indicator measuring the share of children and adolescents living in single-parent households in the planning area at the reference date |
| `s3_transfer_benefit_status_indicator` | status indicator measuring the share of persons receiving transfer benefits in the planning area at the reference date |
| `s4_child_poverty_status_indicator` | status indicator measuring the share of children affected by poverty in the planning area at the reference date |
| `d1_unempl_dynamics_indicator` | dynamics indicator measuring the change in the unemployment share over the observation period (2 years) |
| `d2_single_parent_child_dynamics_indicator` | dynamics indicator measuring the change in the share of children and adolescents living in single-parent households over the observation period (2 years) |
| `d3_transfer_benefit_dynamics_indicator` | dynamics indicator measuring the change in the share of persons receiving transfer benefits over the observation period (2 years) |
| `d4_child_poverty_dynamics_indicator` | dynamics indicator measuring the change in the share of children affected by poverty over the observation period (2 years) |

## `mss_context_x.csv`

| Variable | Explanation |
|---|---|
| `plr_key` | number of the Planungsraum (8-digits) |
| `plr_name` | name of the Planungsraum |
| `res_count` | count of residents |
| `k1_youth_unempl_sgb2_share_pct` | share of unemployed persons receiving benefits under SGB II who are under 25 years old, measured as a percentage of the population aged 15 to under 25 |
| `k2_single_parent_hh_with_children_share_pct` | share of single-parent households with children among all households with children, in percent |
| `k3_old_age_basic_income_support_share_pct` | share of people at or above the statutory retirement age receiving basic income support under SGB XII, Chapter 4, outside institutions, in percent |
| `k18_fem_old_age_basic_income_support_share_pct` | share of women above the statutory retirement age receiving old-age basic income support under SGB XII, Chapter 4, outside institutions, measured as a percentage of the female population above the statutory retirement age |
| `k19_male_old_age_basic_income_support_share_pct` | share of men above the statutory retirement age receiving old-age basic income support under SGB XII, Chapter 4, outside institutions, measured as a percentage of the male population above the statutory retirement age |
| `k4_child_adol_migr_bckgr_share_pct` | share of people under 18 with a migration background among all people under 18, in percent |
| `k5_res_migr_bckgr_share_pct` | share of residents with a migration background among all residents, in percent |
| `k16_foreign_res_share_pct` | share of foreign residents among all residents, in percent |
| `k6_foreign_res_share_change_2yr_pp` | change in the share of foreign residents among all residents over a two-year period, measured in percentage points |
| `k17_non_eu_foreign_res_share_pct` | share of non-EU foreign residents among all foreign residents, in percent |
| `k7_foreign_sgb2_transfer_recipients_share_pct` | share of foreign persons living in SGB II benefit communities among foreign residents below the statutory retirement age, in percent |
| `k8_municipal_housing_units_share_pct` | share of housing units owned by state-owned housing companies among all housing units, in percent |
| `k14_avg_rooms_per_res` | average number of rooms, including kitchens, per resident |
| `k15_avg_living_space_sqm_per_res` | average living space in square metres per resident |
| `k9_res_simple_resid_loc_share_pct` | share of residents living in simple residential locations, including areas affected by road traffic noise, among all residents, in percent |
| `k10_res_same_addr_5plus_years_share_pct` | share of residents who have lived at the same address for at least five years among residents aged five and older, in percent |
| `k11_migr_vol_2yr_avg_annual_pct` | average migration volume over a two-year period, calculated as in-migration plus out-migration per 100 residents, expressed as an annual percentage |
| `k12_net_migr_total_2yr_avg_annual_pct` | average total net migration over a two-year period, calculated as in-migration minus out-migration per 100 residents, expressed as an annual percentage |
| `k13_net_migr_under_6_2yr_avg_annual_pct` | average net migration of children under 6 over a two-year period, calculated as in-migration minus out-migration of children under 6 per 100 residents under 6, expressed as an annual percentage |
| `date` | day, month and year of data (reference date) |
